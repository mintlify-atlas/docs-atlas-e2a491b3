Estudio Radio América is ready to use the moment you open it in a browser — no account or download required for listeners. This guide walks you through the main features available to end users and the steps content managers need to log in and start publishing.

## Tune in as a listener

### Access the platform

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the platform">
    Navigate to the Estudio Radio América URL in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge). The page loads with a full-screen hero section showing the current featured or live content.

    <Tip>
      The platform saves your light/dark mode preference in `localStorage`. It will remember your choice on your next visit.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tune into live radio">
    The **persistent player bar** is fixed to the bottom of the screen on every page. It shows "Radio América 90.9 FM" with a pulsing live indicator.

    - Click the **play button** (circle in the center of the bar) to start streaming.
    - Click it again to pause.
    - On desktop, drag the **volume slider** on the right side of the bar to adjust the audio level. The default volume is 80%.

    <Note>
      The live radio stream at `https://transmision.radioamerica.com.ve:8087/RA909FM` runs continuously. You can navigate to any page on the platform and the audio keeps playing uninterrupted.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse featured content on the home page">
    The hero carousel at the top of the home page rotates through live or featured videos every 7 seconds. Each slide shows:

    - A **title** and **description** for the featured item.
    - A badge — **EN VIVO** (red, animated) for live content, or **Especial** for featured pre-recorded content.
    - An **action button** — click **SINTONIZAR AHORA** for live content or **ESCUCHAR AHORA** for an on-demand episode to go directly to the player.

    Below the hero, the **Programas Destacados** carousel scrolls horizontally through all available programs. Hover over the carousel to pause the auto-scroll, then click any program card to open that program's page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Watch a video or listen to a podcast">
    Any time you click a play button or a video card, the platform navigates to `/watch/:id` where the full player loads.

    On the watch page you can:

    - Play and pause the video or audio episode.
    - Use the timeline scrubber to seek within the episode.
    - HLS streams (`.m3u8`) play with the built-in hls.js player; standard MP4 files and external links play with the native browser player.

    <Info>
      HLS content is protected — right-click and the download option are disabled for `.m3u8` streams. MP4 files and external links do not carry this restriction.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse all programs">
    Click **Programas Destacados** in the top navigation bar, or go directly to `/programas` in a new tab. The programs page lists every show and podcast in the catalog.

    Each program card displays:

    - The program thumbnail (poster image).
    - The program name and category badge.

    Click any card to open that program's episode list.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Subscribe to the newsletter">
    Scroll to the **Únete a la Comunidad** section near the bottom of the home page.

    1. Enter your email address in the input field.
    2. Click **SUSCRIBIRME**.
    3. A confirmation alert will appear. Check your inbox (or spam folder) for the welcome email.

    <Note>
      If your email is already subscribed, the platform will let you know instead of creating a duplicate entry.
    </Note>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Log in as a content manager

Content managers access the Estudio Radio América admin dashboard through a separate login form. Only users with the correct admin credentials can sign in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the login page">
    Navigate to `/login` in your browser. You will see the admin sign-in form.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Enter the **email address** and **password** associated with your admin account, then click **INGRESAR AL PANEL**.

    <Warning>
      Admin credentials are managed by your system administrator. Contact them if you need access or a password reset.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Access the admin dashboard">
    After a successful login you are redirected to `/admin`. From the dashboard you can:

    - **Content management** — upload, edit, and delete videos, programs, and episodes.
    - **Sponsors** — register and manage sponsor spots linked to programs.
    - **Analytics** — view engagement data for your content.
    - **Station profile** — update the station's name, bio, avatar, and social media links (YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X).

    <Tip>
      Changes you publish in the admin dashboard appear on the public-facing site immediately — no rebuild or deployment is required.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>
