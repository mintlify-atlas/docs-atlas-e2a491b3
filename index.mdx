Estudio Radio América is the official streaming platform for **Radio América 90.9 FM**, Venezuela's historic broadcast network. The platform lets listeners tune in to the live radio stream, watch on-demand video interviews and documentaries, follow their favorite programs and podcasts, and discover short-form content — all from a single, beautifully designed interface.

## Getting started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Access the platform">
    Visit the Estudio Radio América web app. The live radio stream starts automatically from the persistent player bar at the bottom of the screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse content">
    Scroll the home page to find featured videos, upcoming releases, and the programs carousel. Click any card to open the full player.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Subscribe to the newsletter">
    Enter your email in the newsletter section at the bottom of the home page to receive notifications when new content is published.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Admin access">
    Content managers can log in at `/login` with their credentials to access the full CMS dashboard for uploading and managing content.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you're a developer integrating with the Estudio Radio América backend, head straight to the [API Reference](/api/overview) to explore all available endpoints.
</Note>
